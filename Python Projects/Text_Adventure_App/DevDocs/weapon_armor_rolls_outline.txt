=== Weapon ===

Name
Damage Bonus
Damage Type (if applicable)
Gold Value

Player character will start with a basic weapon, providing no bonus
Weapons will give a small bonus and/or different damage type when applicable
Ex sword is slashing, mace is bludgeoning

=== Armor ===

Name
Armor Value
Damage Protection Type (if applicable)
Gold Value

Player character will start with basic armor providing no bonus
Armor will protect against some damage and/or different damage types when applicable
Ex light armor provides less protection than heavy, metal provides different damage reduction than leather

== Stat Rolls ==

Strength = 1d6 + 6
HP = 1d6 + 12
Luck = 1d6 + 6

Strength is overall damage dealt by weapons, weapons will give a small bonus and/or different damage type when applicable
Max Health is the maximum health available, base value cannot be increased
Luck can be used in battle and when asked to Test Your Lock. Each use decreases the luck score by 1. To use luck, roll 2d6 and if the players
    luck score is higher, than they succeed. In combat, when dealing damage or taking damage, the player can test their luck to increase or reduce
    the damage

== Provisions ==

Begin with 10 provisions, may rest when given the chance (for now) and eat 1 meal, each meal restores 4 HP

== Potions ==

Start with 1 of 3 potions, Health/Strength/Luck

- Health Potion: restores health to initial max HP
- Strength Potion: restores strength to initial max STR
- Luck Potion: restores luck to initial max Luck + 1 to max

Starting potions can be used twice

== Combat ==

Player character/NPC combatant each roll 2d6 + the player/NPC Strength
Add any increases/decreases from weapon, armor, and damage type resistance if applicable
Formula = 2d6 + STR + WeapBonus - Armor - DmgRes
The higher value wins and deals 2 damage
When dealing/taking damage, we can now use luck to increase/decrease the damage
