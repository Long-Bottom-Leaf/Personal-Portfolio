Founded by the Maserati brothers in 1914, the Maserati company have been building quality vehicles since 1926. First developing cars for street racing, Maserati has since focused on high end vehicles that bridges the gap between racing performance and luxury vehicles. Modern day Maserati continues to innovate on road-going grand tourers and boasts a catalogue of quality cars such as the GranTurismo, the Levante, and the Quattroporte, achieving over 26,600 units sold in 2023.
