Design Specifications

The Maserati GranTurismo combines Italian elegance, innovative technology, and exhilarating performance in a truly unforgettable drive.

Comfort & Craftsmanship

Designed for long journeys in style, the GranTurismo offers refined comfort and an elegant cabin. A 33” digital display setup and a 12.2” customizable digital dashboard provide cutting-edge control and personalization.

Immersive Technology

The Maserati Intelligent Assistant enables intuitive interaction via voice, touch, or gestures. An audio system by Sonus faber delivers a concert-like experience with custom subwoofer technology.

Performance Options

The GranTurismo is powered by the 3.0L V6 Nettuno twin-turbo engine, featuring Formula 1-inspired pre-chamber combustion:

  • 490 CV in the standard GranTurismo
  • 550 CV in the high-performance Trofeo

Folgore: The All-Electric GranTurismo

Powered by three 300-kW motors and 800-volt Formula E-derived technology, the Folgore delivers up to 761 CV of electric performance. A 92.5 kWh battery and 560 kW discharge capacity ensure sustained speed and acceleration, enhanced by a low-profile battery layout.
