	Brief Intro:

Founded by the Maserati brothers in 1914, the Maserati company have been building quality vehicles since 1926. First developing cars for street racing, Maserati has since focused on high end vehicles that bridges the gap between racing performance and luxury vehicles. Modern day Maserati continues to innovate on road-going grand tourers and boasts a catalogue of quality cars such as the GranTurismo, the Levante, and the Quattroporte, achieving over 26,600 units sold in 2023.

Experience the thrill of grand touring with luxurious comfort for all four passengers. The Maserati GranTurismo combines Italian elegance, innovative technology, and exhilarating performance in a truly unforgettable drive.

Comfort & Craftsmanship
Designed for long journeys in style, the GranTurismo offers refined comfort and an elegant cabin crafted from noble materials. A 33” digital display setup and a 12.2” customizable digital dashboard provide cutting-edge control and personalization.

Immersive Technology
The Maserati Intelligent Assistant enables intuitive interaction via voice, touch, or gestures. A bespoke audio system by Sonus faber delivers a concert-like experience with custom subwoofer technology.

Performance Options
The GranTurismo is powered by the 3.0L V6 Nettuno twin-turbo engine, featuring Formula 1-inspired pre-chamber combustion:

490 CV in the standard GranTurismo

550 CV in the high-performance Trofeo

Folgore: The All-Electric GranTurismo
Powered by three 300-kW motors and 800-volt Formula E-derived technology, the Folgore delivers up to 761 CV of fully electric performance. A 92.5 kWh battery and 560 kW discharge capacity ensure sustained speed and acceleration, enhanced by a low-profile battery layout.
